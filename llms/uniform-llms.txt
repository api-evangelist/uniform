# Uniform

> Uniform (Uniform Systems, Inc., uniform.dev) is a visual workspace / composable digital experience platform (DXP) that lets teams compose content, commerce, and data from 70+ integrated sources into personalized, A/B-tested digital experiences without glue code. The Uniform Platform API (REST, /api/v1 on uniform.app or eu.uniform.app) manages compositions, entries, content types, components, assets, releases, projects, webhooks, personalization, and AI agents. Recognized as a Visionary in the 2025 Gartner Magic Quadrant for Digital Experience Platforms. Backed by Insight Partners.

Generated by API Evangelist from apis.yml and repo artifacts on 2026-07-21; Uniform publishes no /llms.txt of its own (probed docs.uniform.app and uniform.dev, both 404).
